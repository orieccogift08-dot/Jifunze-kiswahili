LESSON 1

Greetings & Introductions

Learn simple Kiswahili greetings and introduce yourself with confidence.

📖 Vocabulary

JamboHello
Habari?How are you?
NzuriGood / Fine
AsanteThank you
KaribuWelcome
Naitwa...My name is...
Unaitwa nani?What is your name?

🗣️ Practice Dialogue

Teacher: Jambo!

Student: Jambo!

Teacher: Unaitwa nani?

Student: Naitwa John.

Teacher: Habari yako?

Student: Nzuri, asante.

✍️ Your Practice

Complete the sentence:

“My name is Gift.”

🎯 Speak aloud

Introduce yourself using:

Jambo! Naitwa YOUR NAME. Habari yako?
